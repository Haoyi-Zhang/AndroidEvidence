\section{Review Design and Corpus}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Review type, objective, and unit of analysis}

This work is a bounded, claim-centered structured evidence synthesis, not a PRISMA-complete review or prevalence meta-analysis. Its analytic unit is a scholarly work contributing an Android artifact-comparison, component/version, provenance or reproducibility mechanism; an empirical supply-chain or AI-development study; or a structuring review or method source.

The coded unit relates object, observation, transformation model, oracle or policy, claim ceiling, and counter-history. Tool-level coding would hide stages such as module separation, signature construction, and version comparison; feature-level coding alone would also hide that clone pairs, family labels, repositories, signed policies, and rebuilds establish different propositions.

\subsection{Search, snowballing, and corpus construction}

Seeds included the Android repackaging benchmark review~\cite{repack}, the Android TPL review~\cite{tpl}, broader Android surveys~\cite{sufatrio,xuandroid,tamandroid}, and static-analysis/testing reviews~\cite{staticandroid,androidtesting}. Backward and targeted forward snowballing followed supply-chain integrity, SBOMs, reproducible builds and AI-mediated development~\cite{wohlin,kitchenham,brereton}. Wohlin's procedure requires inclusion before seed expansion and admits missed citation clusters; Kitchenham's 2004 report separates retrieval, quality assessment, extraction and synthesis. We use those distinctions, not their examples as validation of this search; independent extraction and a complete search denominator remain unavailable.

A 2024--2026 update added repackaging, TPL and adjacent review records~\cite{wurepack2026,zengtpsurvey2024,reichert2024}. Research-direction and supply-chain reviews~\cite{williams2025,gokkaya2026,cyberrisk2026} and empirical SBOM studies~\cite{bi2024sbom,bomsaway2024} were tracked separately. A candidate SBOM review was excluded after its official record reported withdrawal and no paper was available for coding. The artifact preserves 105 screening decisions plus source-location and retrospective discovery rows for every retained work; these support record audit, not a database-hit denominator.

The map contains \CorpusWorks{} works: \PrimaryWorks{} primary/system and \SecondaryWorks{} review/method records. \PrimaryVenueWorks{} primary/system and \VenueRecordedWorks{} total records are venue-published; the remainder are three preprints and one method report. Legacy depth labels (\FullSelectedWorks{} full-or-selected-source; \AbstractSelectedWorks{} abstract-or-selected-passage) do not certify passages read. The locator ledger records PDF page/section or numbered primary-HTML locations for \SourceCheckedWorks{} works and lacks such checks for \SourceUncheckedWorks{} legacy records; neither count measures complete semantic coding.

The heterogeneous corpus is not pooled into a detector ranking: candidate generation, transformations, labels, periods, and counting units differ, and benchmark construction can dominate apparent results~\cite{repack,repackeval,blackburn}. The synthesis therefore compares mechanisms and inference validity.

\subsection{Corpus records}

The evidence package separates the representation consumed for coding from the canonical bibliographic record. Each retained work has a corpus row, BibTeX entry, source-location boundary, retrospective discovery row, and publication-status record. DOI-bearing entries use the DOI as canonical identifier; other records use an official venue, archive, or report locator. Automated checks require title and year agreement, unique identifiers, and consistency between venue/preprint status and entry type. A withdrawn SBOM candidate is preserved only in an exclusion ledger and historical regression fixture. These checks establish record-level consistency, not a comprehensive retraction-registry search or independent confirmation of every interpretation.

\subsection{Inclusion, exclusion, and source-depth rules}

Works were included when their technical object could support an Android lineage or release claim. Clone/malware systems supplied reusable artifact observations; supply-chain systems supplied source, build, repository, or release evidence; and AI papers generated mobile software or measured security-relevant generation workflows. General model, GUI-agent, or prompt-injection work without a software-artifact lineage object was excluded.

Publication status and evidential role were separate. Preprints expose emerging workflows and method reports guide procedure, while venue status identifies peer-reviewed primary evidence. Every record has a scholarly/official locator and version-status row; known withdrawn or retracted candidates are quarantined. Records without acquired PDFs include an Android review and a watermark study~\cite{sufatrio,droidmarking}, repackaging and library studies~\cite{repdroid,androsimilar,libdx}, a library-detection study~\cite{libroad}, and supply-chain detection/signing records~\cite{ohmstuke,sigstore}. Reichert et al. also remain outside the acquired set~\cite{reichert2024}. Gokkaya et al.'s retrieved v1 has complete scoped coding, with comparison to the differently titled final text still unresolved~\cite{gokkaya2026}. Bibliographic availability and technical reading are tracked separately.

\subsection{Claim-level extraction and recheck}

The seven extraction fields are evidence object, observation, transformation model, oracle or policy, claim ceiling, counter-history, and inference limit. Field population and source location are not semantic reading. Broad categories such as ``renaming or library reuse'' do not mean every cited study tested each operator; counter-histories and inference limits are our classifications. Source-specific interpretations require the study's actual object, transformation, oracle, and version.

For example, FlowDroid analyzes lifecycle-aware taint paths, not renaming robustness~\cite{flowdroid}; LibScout depends on package hierarchy and version-discriminating interfaces~\cite{libscout}; ATVHunter distinguishes library from version identification~\cite{atvhunter}. Legacy category fields cannot establish per-operator robustness.

The cumulative ledger contains \SourceRechecks{} dated same-process locator rechecks (\SourceRecheckPercent\%). A separate reader-assessment ledger records complete retrieved-PDF/seven-field coding for \SemanticReadWorks{} of \CorpusWorks{} works, covering every acquired PDF. Each assessment relates the study's observation, transformation and oracle to the statements used in this synthesis. The remaining \WithoutLocatedCoding{} records have no acquired PDF through the attempted routes, although some have selected HTML readings.

Supplementary tables contain \LocatedRecodedRecords{} located records and \LocatedCodingFields{}/\TotalCodingFields{} locator-to-field bindings. Counts are generated from these exact record sets, with source reports separated from analytical classifications. Complete reading applies to the retrieved body, references and included appendices. Version boundaries remain explicit: RepDetector's author copy differs from its earlier selected HTML representation; MaMaDroid's 2019 extension differs from the conference paper; and AppForge v1 has 21 physical pages. Other author/preprint copies retain their stated final-publication distinctions.

\paragraph{Use of generative AI}
OpenAI Codex assisted retrieval, source reading, seven-field coding, matrix review, locator/field-binding tests and exposition. These same-process assessments and selected-passage cross-checks are not independent second-human coding. Software checks bindings and calculations; the reading judgments remain interpretations of the retrieved sources.

The claim ladder states \emph{which proposition} is supported; an orthogonal grade states \emph{how it was evaluated}.

The two axes must remain separate. A valid signature may strongly bind a key and artifact while saying nothing about functional equivalence. A transformation-aware benchmark can strongly evaluate resemblance robustness without identifying the authorized publisher.

\subsection{Synthesis and contradiction procedure}

Synthesis first grouped observation families, then assigned each a ceiling and counter-history, and finally mapped the bindings needed across the Android lifecycle. Contradictions denote incompatible objects, denominators, times, policies, or oracles rather than numerical disagreement alone. This is not vote counting: repeated observations cannot convert resemblance into direction or provenance into benign behavior. Seven synthesis propositions retain named support, counter-histories, and boundaries in the claim ledger.

\subsection{Deterministic traceability and negative controls}

The artifact tests cross-ledger identities, publication status, source boundaries, dated recoding transitions and generated manuscript totals. Mutation controls exercise invalid changes to keys, titles, dates, fields and excluded-record status. Complete reader assessments are tracked separately from these structural checks.

The retained historical pilot exercises unknown handling against an independently expressed completion rule. Its exhaustive $3\times3$ test covers 19,683 ternary matrices and 262,144 binary completions. This validates bookkeeping semantics; literature interpretation remains a reading judgment. Counts and display rows are regenerated from current ledgers before compilation.

\subsection{Validity limits}

The review is a bounded evidence map with complete seven-field coding for \SemanticReadWorks{} records and unresolved interpretations for the remainder. Its search denominator and independent second coding are unavailable. Detector accuracy and prevalence are not pooled across incompatible study designs; unresolved matrix cells keep literature-wide absence undetermined.
